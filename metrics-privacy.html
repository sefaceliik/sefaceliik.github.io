sefadevlab metrics – Privacy Policy

sefadevlab metrics is a private script used only by its developer, Sefa Çelik. It reads the developer's own AdMob reports (read-only access) on the developer's own computer to track the earnings of his apps.

It does not collect, store or share data about anyone else. Report data stays on the developer's computer and is not sold or sent to third parties.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
